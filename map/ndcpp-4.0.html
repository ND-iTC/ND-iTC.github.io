How to read this

Choose a requirement on the left. You will see the requirements it needs (dependencies listed in Annex E of the cPP) and those that need it, with links to the cPP and the Supporting Document.

Dependency names shown in grey, such as FPT_STM.1, come from the Common Criteria and are satisfied by an extended requirement in this cPP; the cPP's rationale says which.

Whether a selection-based or optional requirement applies to a given product depends on the selections made in its Security Target. The published PDF is authoritative; this map is for navigation.
